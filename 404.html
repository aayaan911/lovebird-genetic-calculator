The People Behind This Tool

This tool wouldn't exist without them

Ayaan Shohan Creator

Owner of KinBird Aviary

Bangladesh

Built this entire calculator from scratch, from the concept and genetics engine to the UI itself. For years, I watched breeders struggle with genetics. There was never a proper tool that actually thought the way real breeders do. So I decided to build one myself. Over the last 20 days and 30+ hours, I engineered every mutation, every pairing, and every edge case from the ground up, then tested it against real breeding logic and real-world outcomes to ensure the calculator reflects how real breeders think and work in practice. Every hour I spent building this, I kept thinking about the breeders on the other side of the screen. This is my way of giving back to the community that taught me everything I know.

Dirk Van den Abeele Scientific Reference

Belgian aviculturist and ornithologist

Belgium

Belgian aviculturist and ornithologist, founder of Ornitho-Genetics VZW. His 2016 Lovebird Compendium, a 769-page scientific reference covering every known mutation, is the primary source this calculator's genetics engine was built and cross-referenced against. A member of MUTAVI Research & Advice Group since 1999 and supervisor of the first complete genome mapping of Agapornis roseicollis. Without his decades of research, tools like this one simply would not exist.

Ornitho-Genetics VZW
Arifin Sumon Contributor

Bangladesh

Widely regarded as one of Bangladesh's most experienced Fischer's lovebird geneticists, with over a decade of hands-on breeding experience across Opaline, Aqua, Pale Fallow, and rare mutation combinations. Cross-validated the calculator's sex-linked inheritance outcomes and reviewed complex multi-mutation edge cases. When breeders across Bangladesh encounter a pairing they can't work out, they ask him.

Adnan Younus Contributor

Pakistan

One of Pakistan's leading specialists in Aqua and Parblue Fischer's lovebird genetics. Provided hands-on cross-validation for the calculator's Aqua series, Aqua B1, Aqua B2, Aqua Homo, and Parblue combinations, catching edge cases in multi-mutation pairings that pure theoretical modelling would have missed. His real-world verification is why the Aqua series results can be trusted.
