# Security and error reporting

## Reporting a security issue

If you find a security problem in lovebirdgenetics.com, please report it
privately rather than opening a public issue.

Contact KinBird Aviary through https://lovebirdgenetics.com and include:

- what you found
- the URL or file involved
- the steps to reproduce it
- what an attacker could do with it

You will get an acknowledgement. Please give a reasonable window for a fix
before disclosing publicly.

## Scope

In scope: the live site at lovebirdgenetics.com and the code in this
repository.

Out of scope: findings that require physical access to a user's device, social
engineering, denial of service by volume, and reports generated by an automated
scanner with no demonstrated impact.

## Reporting a genetics error

**Genetics errors are treated as seriously as security issues, and they are
more common.**

If the calculator predicts something you believe is wrong, or an article states
something contradicted by the published record, please say so. Include the
pairing or the claim, what you expected, and the source you are relying on.

Where a correction is supported by published work, it is applied and credited.
This site has previously rewritten its entire treatment of a mutation across
six files on the strength of one such correction.
