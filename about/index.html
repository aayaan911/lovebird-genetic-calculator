About the Lovebird Genetics Calculator

lovebirdgenetics.com is a free, browser-based genetics calculator built specifically for Agapornis fischeri. It predicts the offspring distribution, by mutation and by sex, for any parent pairing, handling up to six traits per parent simultaneously.

Most existing lovebird calculators were built for Agapornis roseicollis (Peach-faced lovebird) or handle a limited mutation set. Fischer's lovebirds have distinct genetics, particularly for Opaline, Ino, and Aqua, and needed a dedicated tool.

Mutations covered:

Aqua B1 Aqua B2 Aqua Homo Opaline Pale Fallow Blue 1 Blue 2 Parblue Yellow Face Pallid Cinnamon Ino / Lutino / Albino Dun Fallow Bronze Fallow Dilute Red Factor Euwing Greywing Misty

The calculator correctly models SF (Single Factor) and DF (Double Factor) outcomes for autosomal dominant incomplete mutations, Euwing, Greywing, and Misty, which most tools either omit or handle incorrectly. Sex-linked mutations (Opaline, Pallid, Cinnamon) show separate male and female outcome columns so breeding plans are immediately actionable.

Scientific basis

Every inheritance model in the calculator maps directly to The Lovebird Compendium by Dirk Van den Abeele (2016, 769 pages), the primary peer-reviewed scientific reference for Agapornis genetics. Dirk Van den Abeele is a biologist, aviculturist, and was supervisor of the first complete genome mapping of Agapornis roseicollis. His compendium documents gene loci, allele relationships, and inheritance mechanisms for all known mutations across the eye-ring and rosy-faced species groups.

Where the Compendium documents species-specific differences between Fischer's and Peach-faced lovebirds, for example, Opaline and Ino, which are sex-linked in roseicollis but autosomal recessive in fischeri, the calculator follows the fischeri model. Pairing outcomes have been validated against documented breeding results contributed by specialist breeders in Bangladesh, Pakistan, and Indonesia.
