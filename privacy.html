Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026  ·  KinBird Aviary

This privacy policy describes how LoveBird Genetic Calculator (lovebirdgenetics.com) collects and uses information when you use our tool.

What We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. However, we use the following third-party analytics tools that may collect usage data automatically:

  • Google Analytics 4: collects anonymous page views, session duration, device type, and general geographic region (country level). No personally identifiable information is stored.
  • Microsoft Clarity: records anonymous session heatmaps and click patterns, plus tags for which mutations and pairing you selected in the calculator, to help us improve the tool's usability. No names, emails, or personal data are captured.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact details
  • No account registration or login, the tool requires no sign-up
  • No payment information
  • No breeding records, pedigrees or bird identities. Every genetics calculation runs entirely in your browser and no result is stored on any server. The anonymous analytics below do log which mutations and which pairing you selected, as an unnamed usage event, so we can see which parts of the calculator get used

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity use cookies and similar tracking technologies to distinguish repeat visits. By using this site, you consent to the use of these analytics cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your Theme Preference

Your dark/light mode preference is stored in your browser's localStorage. A one-time session flag (to control the theme button animation) is stored in sessionStorage and clears automatically when you close the tab. Neither value is ever transmitted to us, both stay entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

This tool is a general-purpose educational calculator. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

The People Behind This Tool

Ayaan Shohan Creator

Owner of KinBird Aviary

Bangladesh

Built this entire calculator from scratch, from the concept and genetics engine to the UI itself. For years, I watched breeders struggle with genetics. There was never a proper tool that actually thought the way real breeders do. So I decided to build one myself. Over the last 20 days and 30+ hours, I engineered every mutation, every pairing, and every edge case from the ground up, then tested it against real breeding logic and real-world outcomes to ensure the calculator reflects how real breeders think and work in practice. Every hour I spent building this, I kept thinking about the breeders on the other side of the screen. This is my way of giving back to the community that taught me everything I know.

Dirk Van den Abeele Scientific Reference

Belgian aviculturist and ornithologist

Belgium

Belgian aviculturist and ornithologist, founder of Ornitho-Genetics VZW. His 2016 Lovebird Compendium, a 769-page scientific reference covering every known mutation, is the primary source this calculator's genetics engine was built and cross-referenced against. A member of MUTAVI Research & Advice Group since 1999 and supervisor of the first complete genome mapping of Agapornis roseicollis.
