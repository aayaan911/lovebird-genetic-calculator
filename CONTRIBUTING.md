# Contributing

This project is not open source, and pull requests that add or change features
will not be merged. Please read the LICENSE before opening one.

What is genuinely wanted is different, and more valuable.

## Genetics corrections

**This is the contribution that matters.** If the engine or an article gets
the genetics wrong, report it.

The site follows the nomenclature published by Dirk Van den Abeele and
Ornitho-Genetics VZW. A correction lands fastest when it includes:

- the exact pairing, or the exact sentence you are disputing
- the gene symbols involved, for example `ino pe`, `ino pd`, `bl aq`
- what the result should be, and why
- the published source, ideally OGVZW, MutaBase or the Lovebird Compendium

Test-mating records from your own aviary are especially welcome. Real breeding
data outranks argument.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with the browser, the device, the pairing that triggered it, and
what you expected instead.

## Translations

Breeders in Indonesia, Bangladesh, Pakistan, India, the Philippines and the
Gulf make up most of the people using this tool. Offers to translate articles
into those languages are welcome. Contact through the site first so the work is
not duplicated.

## Ground rules

Corrections are credited. Disagreements about genetics are settled by evidence,
not by seniority. If you are right, the site changes.
